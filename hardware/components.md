## Hardware Configuration

The power stage uses a P-channel MOSFET driven through a 2N2222 transistor. The MOSFET is controlled by a 20 kHz PWM signal generated by the Arduino Uno.

A Schottky diode, inductor and output capacitor form the buck converter power stage.

Output voltage is measured through a 15 kΩ / 10 kΩ resistor divider connected to analog input A0.

The Arduino implements closed-loop PI voltage regulation, duty-cycle limiting and software overvoltage detection.

## Hardware Considerations

During experimental testing, switching-related interference affected ADC voltage measurements.

Improving the grounding arrangement eliminated the observed measurement spikes in the diagnostic test.

This highlighted the importance of proper grounding, wiring layout and noise management in switching power converters.
